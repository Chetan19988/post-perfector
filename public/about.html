About Chetan Crane Service Pvt Ltd

Pioneering Heavy Lifting & Material Handling Excellence Across Delhi NCR.

Who We Are

Chetan Crane Service Pvt Ltd (operating under the brand Chetan Lifters) is one of Delhi NCR’s most trusted industrial forklift rental and crane handling partners. Headquartered at our strategic Ghazipur Base near the Delhi-UP border, we specialize in high-capacity diesel forklift rentals complete with certified, experienced operators.

Our Core Fleet

We maintain a modern fleet of well-serviced diesel forklifts ranging from 3 Ton, 5 Ton, to 10 Ton capacities. Every unit undergoes rigorous safety checks before dispatch to ensure zero downtime at your factory, warehouse, or job site.

Why Choose Ghazipur Base?

Our central Ghazipur location allows rapid mobilization to all key industrial hubs across the region:

  • East, South, North & Central Delhi
  • Noida, Greater Noida & Expressway Belt
  • Ghaziabad, Sahibabad & Loni Areas
  • Gurgaon, Manesar & Faridabad
